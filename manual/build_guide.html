Before you start
Print
Shell upright, ring upside down (top face on the bed), plate face down, cap pocket down; 0.2 mm layers, four walls. Print the bezel in clear resin: its flange cannot be bridged and its fits are 0.25 mm. Print a hole coupon before trusting the insert holes.
Panel first
Buy two panels. Before anything else, look at the flex: after it folds under the glass, pin 1 must land on the 3 o'clock side of the board's pads. If yours is mirrored, the board needs regenerating with FPC_PIN1_RIGHT flipped, not a different fold.
Battery
The cell is live whatever the switch does. It is plugged in only after the soldering (step 9), and the insulation sheet goes on it before the board. Check the lead's polarity with the meter; many cells are wired the other way round.
Iron
220 °C for the inserts, 320 °C with plenty of flux for the 0.7 mm flex. Clean with alcohol.
Compass
Keep steel away from the compass: aluminium bolts only, no steel screws or magnets near the puck.

Done
The orange dot is 12 o'clock. The arrow points at your partner and the blip sits at their range on the rings; the scale (50 m, 500 m, 5 km) is printed at 3 o'clock. While you hold the button the screen tells you what letting go will do.
The button
Press
Beacon on or off for 60 s: both pucks strobe so your friend can spot you.
Double press
Vibration on or off (remembered).
Hold 2 s, release
Pairing mode. Hold again to cancel.
Hold 5 s, release
Compass calibration: turn the puck through a slow figure of eight.
